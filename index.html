Pre-filing Notice & Completion Checklist
60–90 days before filing the Application with the Board
0/0
▾
Critical Window: The Pre-filing Notice must be filed no less than 60 days and no more than 90 days before submitting the Application. If the Application is not filed within 90 days, a new Pre-filing Notice is required.

Regulation: 980 CMR 16.00 (M.G.L. c. 164, §§69J, 69J¼, 69T, 69U, 69V)  ·  Effective February 27, 2026  ·  Applies to applications filed on or after July 1, 2026
Note: This checklist is for LCTDIF / transmission line rebuilds under §69T. Items tagged 🌿 Environmental involve MEPA, Agency Consultation, or environmental permitting — coordinate with or defer to your Environmental Permitting lead and consultants on these items. Items tagged ⚡ TDIF apply specifically to linear/transmission facilities. For waiver requests, submit in writing to DPP with supporting documentation (§16.03(1)).
